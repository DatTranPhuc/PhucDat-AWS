### Needs Improvement

* **Time management and process discipline:** This is the area I rated myself as average during the FCAJ program. I need to build stronger habits around meeting deadlines and ensuring tasks are completed on schedule, especially when working on multiple parts of the workshop simultaneously.
* **Technical problem-solving mindset:** While building the FCJ Workshop Template, I sometimes spent too much time troubleshooting AWS technical issues (IAM configurations, networking, etc.). I need to improve my ability to identify root causes more quickly and approach problems in a more structured, systematic way.
* **Technical communication and presentation:** I recognize that I still have room to grow in clearly expressing technical ideas — both in written documentation (workshop content) and in direct discussions with the team. I need to practice writing more coherent documentation and build confidence when presenting to the group.
