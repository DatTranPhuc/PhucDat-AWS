# Event Summary: "Cloud Architect Challenge"

&emsp; **Event Name:** Cloud Architect Challenge — FCAJ Opening Activity

&emsp; **Date & Time:** During the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, 2026

&emsp; **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp; **Role:** Participant / Team Member

---

### Event Overview

The **Cloud Architect Challenge** was an interactive team competition held as the opening activity of the FCAJ program. Eight teams competed by answering questions covering a wide range of topics including Cloud Computing fundamentals, AWS services, cloud architecture design, and practical technical scenarios.

The competition ran in an elimination format — after each round, the team with the lowest accumulated score was removed from the contest. This continued until a single winning team remained. The format was fast-paced and demanded both technical knowledge and teamwork under pressure.

---

### Key Highlights

#### Competition Format
- **8 teams** competed simultaneously across multiple rounds
- **Elimination-based** structure — lowest-scoring team exits each round
- Questions spanned: Cloud concepts, AWS service selection, architecture trade-offs, and real-world scenarios
- Required quick consensus within the team before submitting answers

#### What Made It Challenging
- Time constraints forced teams to prioritize and decide fast — no room for lengthy debate
- Some questions involved choosing between AWS services with overlapping functionality (e.g., SQS vs. SNS vs. EventBridge), requiring nuanced understanding
- Watching other teams answer — and hearing the post-round explanations — revealed gaps in my own knowledge I hadn't noticed before

---

### What I Learned

- **Solidified AWS fundamentals:** The pressure of the competition forced me to recall and apply core AWS concepts quickly, rather than just passively reading documentation.
- **Learned from other teams:** Hearing different approaches from competing teams was just as valuable as the official explanations — it showed that there are multiple valid ways to design a cloud solution.
- **Identified knowledge gaps:** Several questions on areas like cost optimization strategies and multi-region design highlighted areas I needed to study more deeply.
- **Teamwork under pressure:** Even with a tight deadline, the team had to communicate clearly and reach consensus. This mirrors real-world cloud architecture decision-making.

---

### Personal Reflection

The Cloud Architect Challenge was a genuinely fun and stimulating way to kick off the FCAJ program. Unlike traditional classroom reviews, the competitive format created a sense of urgency that made every question feel more meaningful.

What stood out most was the moment after each round when the facilitator explained the correct answers — those explanations were often the most educational part, because they went beyond "what is correct" to explain *why*, including the architectural reasoning and trade-offs involved.

I came away with a renewed motivation to go deeper into AWS services I had only surface-level knowledge of before, especially around networking, cost management, and serverless patterns.
