# Event Summary: "FCAJ x FPTU Cloud Mastery — AI From Scratch"

&emsp; **Event Name:** FCAJ x FPTU Cloud Mastery — AI From Scratch

&emsp; **Date & Time:** During the FCAJ program, 2026

&emsp; **Location:** FPT University, Ho Chi Minh City (In-person)

&emsp; **Role:** Attendee

---

### Event Overview

**FCAJ x FPTU Cloud Mastery — AI From Scratch** was a collaborative event between the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program and FPT University. The event brought together cloud and AI practitioners to share their experience directly with students, covering how modern AI solutions are built, deployed, and operated on cloud infrastructure.

Three speakers from the industry led the sessions:
- **Nguyen Tuan Thinh** — DevSecOps Engineer, STYL Solution
- **Huynh An Khuong** — Full-Stack Developer, VALSEA
- **Nguyen Tran Minh Quan** — MLOps Engineer, VALSEA

What made this event stand out was that it was held on campus — giving students a chance to interact directly with practitioners and ask questions in real time, rather than watching remotely.

---

### Key Highlights

#### Topics Covered
- **AI fundamentals and practical applications** — the speakers broke down how AI concepts translate into real systems, beyond just theory
- **Cloud as the backbone of AI deployment** — how platforms like AWS serve as the infrastructure layer that makes AI solutions scalable and production-ready
- **System design for AI workloads** — key considerations for building systems that are flexible, efficient, and easy to maintain as data and usage grow
- **DevSecOps in AI pipelines** — security and operational practices that need to be built into AI systems from the start, not added afterward
- **MLOps in practice** — how models go from experiments to production deployments, and what it takes to maintain them reliably over time

#### What Made It Memorable
- The hands-on demo that caught everyone's attention was a browser-based fighting game where players controlled characters using real-time multiplayer connections — a fun and creative way to showcase cloud-powered real-time communication in action
- The mix of speakers from DevSecOps, Full-Stack, and MLOps roles showed that building AI products requires collaboration across very different skill sets
- Having the event on the FPT campus made it more accessible and created a more relaxed atmosphere for open discussion

---

### What I Learned

- **AI development is a team sport** — a production-ready AI system requires contributions from engineers across security, infrastructure, application, and data science disciplines working together
- **Cloud scalability is a prerequisite for AI, not a bonus** — the ability to scale compute and storage on demand is what separates a working prototype from a production-grade AI application
- **Security must be designed into AI pipelines from day one** — the DevSecOps perspective showed me that late-stage security fixes are far more costly than building secure-by-default systems
- **MLOps bridges the gap between research and production** — without a proper MLOps pipeline, models may work in notebooks but fail in real deployment environments
- **Practical demos communicate better than slides** — seeing the real-time game demo made the concept of cloud-powered low-latency communication click instantly in a way that diagrams alone couldn't

---

### Personal Reflection

This was the first event I attended in person during the FCAJ program, and the experience was noticeably different from online sessions. Being in the same room as the speakers made it easier to follow along, ask questions naturally, and pick up on the subtleties of how they discussed technical trade-offs.

What I appreciated most was how grounded the content was. The speakers weren't selling a vision — they were sharing specific problems they had solved and specific decisions they had made. That kind of firsthand perspective is something I can't get from documentation or tutorials alone.

The live demo of the cloud-powered fighting game was an unexpected highlight. It reminded me that cloud infrastructure isn't just for enterprise backends — it can enable creative, interactive, real-time experiences that wouldn't be possible without it.
